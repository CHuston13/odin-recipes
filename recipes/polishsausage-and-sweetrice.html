Polish Sausage and sweet rice

A delicious sweet treat full of protein
  • 1 cup rice of your choosing
  • Polish sausage
  • Tablespoon of butter
  • Two table spoons sugar
  1. First wash rice until water is clear
  2. Cook rice in rice maker or on stove until cooked
  3. Cook sausage until brown on inside
  4. Add butter and sugar to Rice. Then mix together
