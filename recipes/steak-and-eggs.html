Steak and Eggs


A delicious combination of steak and eggs. A hearty and healthy breakfast

Ingredients

  • 2 steaks about 1 inch thick
  • 4-8 large eggs per person
  • 2 table spoons ghee or butter
  • 1 teaspoon salt, ½ teaspoon black pepper, and 1 teaspoon garlic powder
  • Freshly chopped parsley or chives

Steps

  1. Prep the steak: Pat the steaks dry with a paper towel. Season both sides generously with salt, pepper, and garlic powder. Let them sit at room temperature for 10 minutes.
  2. Sear the steak: Heat a large cast-iron skillet over medium-high heat and add 1 tablespoon of ghee or butter. Place the steaks in the hot pan and sear for 3 to 4 minutes per side for medium-rare (reaching an internal temperature of 120°F–130°F).
  3. • Rest the meat: Remove the steaks from the skillet and place them on a cutting board or warm plate. Cover them loosely with foil and let them rest for 5 minutes so the juices redistribute.
  4. Cook the eggs: Wipe out any excess charred bits from the skillet with a paper towel. Reduce the heat to medium and add the remaining tablespoon of ghee or butter. Crack the eggs into the pan and season them lightly with a pinch of salt and pepper. Cook for 2 to 3 minutes until the whites are fully set and the yolks reach your preferred doneness (sunny-side up or over-easy).
  5. Slice and serve: Slice the rested steak against the grain into thin strips. Serve the warm steak alongside the freshly cooked eggs, garnished with chopped parsley.
